MCP servers

Ask the system in your own words. Press once.

The assistant reads as the user and prepares the work

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard that lets AI assistants work with business systems. We build the MCP server around your roles and flows: sales asks about orders and stock, the planner about capacity, purchasing about late deliveries. You can own the solution yourselves.

  • Sign-in with your existing account. No passwords with us.
  • Read tools that follow the system's own permissions.
  • Changes are prepared as a sheet and sent only when a person presses.
  • Everything is logged: what was prepared, who approved, what the system answered.

ERP integration

Everything your business system can do, on a modern page.

The logic stuck in the desktop client becomes readable everywhere

Business rules, validations, print layouts and workflows built up over the years only work in a client on one PC. We import them, translate them into plain language and make them available on the web, on mobile and for AI.

  • Lists and cards that look like the system's own forms, so nobody has to relearn.
  • Pick and pack, purchasing, returns and approvals as workflows.
  • Documents in the customer's language and currency, as PDF, email or print.
MRP and production planning

A plan that says why.

Explained recommendations, scenarios and delivery dates

Classic MRP gives a list of recommendations and leaves the planner to guess. We show the need behind every line: which order, which bill of materials and which stock level drove it, and let the planner ask "what happens if the delivery is two weeks late?".

  • Purchase and production recommendations with their reason.
  • Capacity per work station, and late orders.
  • Can we deliver, and when, straight to the salesperson.

How we work

Close, in small steps, with you in the room.

We are the same people in the first call, in the build and in operation. Scope, approach and terms we decide together with you, not in advance.

  1. First call

    You tell us what you want to achieve. We show a clickable prototype of your own flow, often before the meeting.

  2. Walkthrough

    We go through your systems, your API and your sign-in together with you, and agree on what to build and in what order.

  3. Build against test

    The solution is built against your test system, in steps you can try. Nothing goes live until you have seen it work.

  4. Operation

    We run the solution, or help you run it yourselves, and keep it in step with your systems and the AI assistants.
